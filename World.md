#### Gambling
Wyvern: Poker
- ante 1 GP
- roll 3d6
- betting like poker
- each round show dice of choice
- After showing third dice, whoever has highest sum wins pot
Duster: Blackjack
- Rules: 
	- Bet
	- Roll 2d10, dealer rolls d20
	- Can double after initial cast
	- Can keep hitting d10 until player busts at 21+
	- Dealer rolls until either
		- He busts at 22+, stop hitting at 17
	- Bust if sum over 21
Miner's dice: game of chance, play against house
- choose 1-6
- dealer choose 3d6
	- if 1 matches, get money back
	- if 2 match, get 2x money back
	- if 3 match, get 3x back
